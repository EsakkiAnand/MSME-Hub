# MSME-Hub Glossary

A unified platform connecting verified MSMEs, academic institutions, and alumni mentors to solve real-world industry problems and incubate student innovation.

## Language

### Roles & Actors

**MSME Admin**:
The governing authority with ecosystem-wide oversight responsible for verifying industry registrations and granting final approval on completed solutions.
_Avoid_: Superuser, platform manager, university admin

**Industry Partner**:
A verified representative of a registered MSME or enterprise who submits industrial problems and reviews delivered solutions.
_Avoid_: Client, sponsor, company user, vendor

**Staff**:
A faculty member or academic supervisor who reviews incoming industry problems, creates projects, assigns student teams, and reviews independent student ideas.
_Avoid_: Teacher, professor, instructor, project manager

**Student**:
An enrolled learner who collaborates on staff-assigned projects, receives mentorship, submits GitHub solution deliverables, and submits independent ideas.
_Avoid_: Trainee, intern, candidate

**Alumni Mentor**:
A graduated alumnus who provides structured, domain-specific technical feedback and architectural critique on active student projects.
_Avoid_: Advisor, external reviewer, alumni user

---

### Core Artifacts & Concepts

**Problem**:
An authentic industrial or operational bottleneck submitted by a verified Industry Partner for academic resolution.
_Avoid_: Ticket, task, issue, challenge

**Project**:
A formalized work stream created by Staff from an accepted Problem or approved Student Idea, staffed with multiple students.
_Avoid_: Assignment, course work, gig

**Solution**:
The final deliverable package submitted by the assigned student team for a project, centered on a verified GitHub repository URL.
_Avoid_: Code submission, homework, pull request

**Idea**:
An independent innovation concept or product proposal submitted by a Student for Staff review and potential project incubation.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, pitch, student project

**Project Team Lead**:
The designated Student within a project team who holds exclusive authority to submit and modify the official solution deliverable and GitHub repository link.
_Avoid_: Captain, admin student, group head

**Guidance Request**:
A direct mentorship inquiry initiated by an assigned student to an Alumni Mentor seeking feedback or architectural critique.
_Avoid_: Help ticket, query, message

**Guidance Board**:
A centralized, tagged institutional board where student teams broadcast guidance queries to all registered Alumni Mentors.
_Avoid_: Forum, help desk, public questions

**Deadline Extension Request**:
A structured request submitted by a Project Team Lead to the Staff mentor for additional calendar time to complete a project deliverable.
_Avoid_: Delay notice, extension form

**Helpful Endorsement**:
A qualitative recognition badge awarded by a Student to an Alumni Mentor upon the resolution of a Guidance Request.
_Avoid_: Upvote, like, review

**Industry Rating**:
A post-approval quantitative and qualitative evaluation (1–5 stars and written review) provided by the Industry Partner upon delivery of an approved solution.
_Avoid_: Grade, score, acceptance sign-off

**Moderated Rating**:
An Industry Rating of 1 or 2 stars held in an administrative review queue by the MSME Admin prior to public portfolio display.
_Avoid_: Disputed review, hidden feedback

**Sponsorship Showcase**:
A curated pool of Staff-accepted student innovation ideas published for verified Industry Partners to review and adopt as sponsored projects.
_Avoid_: Pitch deck, marketplace, idea listing

**Feedback**:
Structured qualitative critique and technical guidance provided by Alumni Mentors or revision instructions provided by the MSME Admin.
_Avoid_: Comment, review note, grading
