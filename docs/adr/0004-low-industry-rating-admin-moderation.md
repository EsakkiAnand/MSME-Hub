# Low Industry Rating Admin Moderation Gate

To protect students from punitive or misaligned 1- and 2-star ratings resulting from industry scope creep rather than poor technical execution, ratings at or below 2 stars are sequestered in an Admin Moderation Queue. The MSME Admin evaluates whether the deliverables met the agreed initial problem specification before deciding to approve public display, mediate revisions, or archive the rating with an internal note.
