# Institutional OAuth and Tiered Role Verification

For Students and Staff, we require authentication through institutional OAuth (e.g., Google Workspace / Microsoft Entra on college email domains) to eliminate fraudulent accounts and streamline login. Industry Partners and Alumni must authenticate through verified credential onboarding, with Industry requiring MSME Admin document verification and Alumni requiring a one-click verification queue before accessing the platform. This establishes trust boundaries without burdening students and faculty with manual account approvals.
